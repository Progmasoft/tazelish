Detailed Description

The command line of Tazelish, read into a typed command. Nothing after the parser looks at the text of an argument again.

◆ SelfAction

enum class Progmasoft::Tazelish::SelfAction
strong

What tazelish self does to Tazelish itself.

Enumerator
kUpdate 

Update Tazelish.

kRemove 

Remove Tazelish and every version of Tazel it installed, and undo the PATH entries it added.

kPurge 

Do what kRemove does, and also delete what Tazelish kept for itself: its config.txt, its download cache and its logs.
